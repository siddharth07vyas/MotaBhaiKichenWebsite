About us

A Local Food Destination in Indore

MOTA BHAI KITCHEN is listed as a vegetarian restaurant on Mahatma Gandhi Road in the Bada Ganpati area of Malharganj, Indore. The menu covers everyday vegetarian favourites – paneer and kaju sabzis, dals, Indore-style sev dishes, seasonal vegetables, rotis and parathas, rice, and Indo-Chinese – along with a Regular Thali.
