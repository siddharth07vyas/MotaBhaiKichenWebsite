About us

A Local Food Destination in Indore

MOTAA BHAI KITCHEN is a pure vegetarian takeaway kitchen on Mahatma Gandhi Road in the Bada Ganpati area of Malharganj, Indore – serving desi flavours and authentic recipes. The menu covers everyday vegetarian favourites – paneer and kaju sabzis, dals, Indore-style sev dishes, seasonal vegetables, rotis and parathas, rice, and Indo-Chinese – along with a Regular Thali.
